The problem

Talent is everywhere. Access isn't.

Many rural schools in South Asia have no computers and no internet. That gap limits access to modern education, digital literacy, and the opportunities that come with them, from higher education to the jobs where tools like Google Docs and spreadsheets are simply expected.

Our solution

Complete, working labs, not just hardware.

We fundraise, purchase fully functional desktop computers and wifi routers, and install complete labs in partnership with trusted on-the-ground organizations. Then we stay involved so the lab keeps teaching.

How it works

Four steps from a fundraiser to a working lab

Step 1

Fundraise

Bake sales, dance events, dinners, and school drives. Every dollar is raised grassroots by students and families.

Step 2

Purchase & Install

We buy fully functional computers and wifi routers and set up a complete, connected lab with our partners.

Step 3

Train & Support

We work alongside teachers to introduce tools like Google Docs, Slides, Khan Academy, and even AI fundamentals.

Step 4

Sustain

We check in with partner schools, follow up on how the lab is used, and keep teaching remotely when we can.
