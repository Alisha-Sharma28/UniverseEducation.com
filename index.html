The problem

Intelligence is everywhere, yet equal access to technology isn't.

Thousands of K–12 schools across rural South Asia still operate offline, without access to any computers or online-learning tools. As the world shifts online, this gap limits students' access to modern education, digital literacy, and equal opportunities to pursue tech-focused careers in the future.

Our solution

We build computer labs where there are none.

We raise the funds, buy the computers and Wi-Fi routers, and work internationally with trusted partners to build fully equipped computer labs at each school. After installation, we conduct monthly surveys to ensure our computers remain in good condition and in active use.

How it works

The four-step procedure to bringing a classroom online

Step 1

Fundraise

Grants, charity auctions, bake sales, and temple partnerships. Each dollar is raised through the support of our community and partners.

Step 2

Research, Purchase, and Install

Through careful research into surrounding communities with limited technology access, we select the schools and educational foundations that we believe will benefit most from a computer lab. Then, we work to find local vendors that offer reliable, fully functional equipment at fair prices. Finally, we work with our chosen vendors to plan the logistics of purchasing, delivery, and installation at each site.

Step 3

Training and Support

After installation, UniVerse Education works alongside teachers at each site, training them on the tools their students need, from Google Docs to Zoom to Khan Academy.

Step 4

Sustain

We periodically check in with schools, track how the lab is being used, and collect student survey data to ensure students are growing their confidence and competence.
