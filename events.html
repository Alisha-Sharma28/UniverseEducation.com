Dinner Night for Education and Hygiene

Co-hosted by UniVerse Education and Owed Soap, two local youth-led nonprofits working to increase education and hygiene access for children and families in underserved rural communities across South Asia and Africa.

Join us for a family dinner night with home-cooked dishes from international cuisines, plus performances, music, and games. Proceeds fund desktop computers for schools and grants that support an ongoing soap supply.
