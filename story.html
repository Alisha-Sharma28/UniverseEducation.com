How it started

A gap that was hard to unsee

UniVerse Education (originally started as Eklipse Education) was founded about two years ago by Balu [Last name], a high school student in San Jose, California. The spark was simple: seeing how many students in South Asia were missing the basic technology access that so many of us take for granted.

[2–3 sentences in the founder's own voice about the personal reason for starting UniVerse Education. For example: a visit, a conversation, or a moment that made the gap real.]Balu [Last name], Founder

The first mission took us to Vizirpur, a small village in Uttar Pradesh, India, where we donated twelve complete desktops to an all-girls school. Since then, every lab has been funded grassroots, one bake sale, dance event, and dinner night at a time.

We believe online technology is essential to student success in the 21st century. The students we work with aren't missing potential. They're missing access.

Partnership

Silicon Valley Youth Lead Partner to Asha for Education

Asha for Education is a global volunteer network dedicated to education for underprivileged children. As a Youth Lead Partner, UniVerse Education holds a lead steward role on two Asha global projects.

The partnership connects us with vetted, on-the-ground organizations and helps make sure every lab we fund lands where it's needed most.
